# Terms and Conditions

*Last updated: 01-01-2026*

## 1. About MUBS Marketplace
MUBS Marketplace is a student-built online marketplace where members of the Makerere University Business School (MUBS) community can advertise and find items and services. It is run by its project team and is not an official service of MUBS unless MUBS states otherwise.

## 2. Who may use it
You must be a MUBS student or staff member with a valid @mubs.ac.ug email address and a registration number. You must give true information. An administrator approves each account and may reject or suspend an account.

## 3. Your account
You are responsible for your account and password. Do not share them. Turn on two-factor authentication to protect your account. Tell an administrator if you think someone else is using your account.

## 4. What you may post
Post only items and services that you own or may lawfully offer, with honest descriptions, prices and photos that are yours. You must not post illegal, stolen, counterfeit, dangerous or adult items, weapons or drugs, or anything abusive or misleading.

## 5. How trading works
MUBS Marketplace only connects buyers and sellers. It does not hold goods, handle payments or deliver items. Buyers and sellers agree on payment and meet in person themselves. Do not send money to someone you have not met.

## 6. Safety
Meet in a public place on campus, preferably in daytime, check the item before paying, and do not share passwords or sensitive personal information. Read the Guidelines page.

## 7. Reporting problems
Use the Report button to report suspicious listings, scams or unsafe behaviour. If you feel unsafe or a crime has happened, contact campus security or the police first, then report it on the system.

## 8. Actions we may take
Administrators may remove listings, reject or suspend accounts, and keep records of reports. Information about an account (name, registration number, email, phone number) may be given to MUBS administration or to the police when it is lawfully requested.

## 9. Responsibility
Trades are between users. The project team does not guarantee any item, user or transaction, and is not responsible for losses between users, to the extent allowed by law.

## 10. Changes and contact
We may update these terms, and will show the date above. Questions: mubsmarketplace@gmail.com.
