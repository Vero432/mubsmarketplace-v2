# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 01-01-2026*

This policy explains what personal information MUBS Marketplace collects and how it is used, in line with the Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 of Uganda.

## 1. What we collect
- **Account details:** name, @mubs.ac.ug email, registration number, phone number, account type (buyer or seller), and a password (stored in a scrambled form).
- **Activity:** listings and photos you post, messages you send inside the system, reports you make, ratings you give, and the time you accepted these terms.
- **Technical data:** session information needed to keep you logged in.

## 2. Why we collect it
To check that users are MUBS members, run the marketplace, let buyers and sellers contact each other, handle reports, and keep users safe. We do not use your information for advertising and we do not sell it.

## 3. Who can see it
- Other users see your name, your listings and your ratings. Your phone number and email are **not** shown on listings or profiles.
- Administrators can see account details so they can approve accounts and handle reports.
- Information may be given to MUBS administration or the police when it is lawfully requested.

## 4. How we protect it
Passwords are stored hashed, forms and pages are protected against common attacks, access is limited by role, and users can turn on two-factor authentication. No system is completely secure, so please keep your password private.
